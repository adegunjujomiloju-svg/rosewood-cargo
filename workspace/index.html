WHO WE ARE

More Than Cargo. We're Your Connection.

Rosewood Cargo Service is a cargo and logistics brand based in Ikorodu, Lagos, Nigeria. We aim to make moving goods easier through dependable service and customer-focused communication.

We believe every shipment matters. That's why our goal is to provide a professional experience from the first conversation to the delivery process.

Our Mission To serve customers with care and commitment.
Our Vision To build lasting connections beyond borders.
